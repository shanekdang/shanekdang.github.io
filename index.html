Hi, I’m Shane — a data scientist with a strong foundation in transportation research, combining over three years of experience in data analysis, programming, and crafting clear, actionable visualizations.

My journey started with a natural curiosity for how things work and, in turn, led to a strong desire to improve how things operate — whether that meant reverse-engineering a software tool or rebuilding parts of my 1992 Mazda MX-5 Miata. That same hands-on mindset drives my professional work, where I thrive on solving complex problems, building efficient workflows, and translating data into stories that people can understand and act on.

With a background that bridges technical execution and direct customer interaction, I value not only clean code and scalable systems, but also human-centered solutions that work in the real world.
